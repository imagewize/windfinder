# Pillar framework — adapted from sergebulaev/linkedin-skills (MIT, pinned 8f8bc4b), condensed

## Why publish from a personal profile, not a company page

Personal profiles reach further than brand pages (employee posts are distributed
6-8x more than brand-page posts in aggregate studies), and buyers check the person
behind the offer before a meeting. Publish on the personal account first; the
company page may reshare, never originate.

## The pillars

### 1. Authority / buyer-pain (40-50% of posts)

Frameworks, tactical how-tos, data-driven takes, industry analysis, teardowns — and
news/rule changes applied to the audience's actual business ("what the new X means
for your shop").

Formats: text-only (tactical), carousel (frameworks), single image.
Hook formulas: F7 Odd-Precision Money, F10 Contrarian + Historical Receipts,
F8 Paid-vs-Free, F5 Self-Proving Meta (see `linkedin-post-writer/references/hook-formulas.md`).

### 2. Personal narrative / behind-the-scenes (30-40%)

Lessons from failures, real client work, tests you ran, what you got wrong. Trust
compounds on specifics; pure insight posts do not land as well.

Formats: long-form text (1,200-1,500 chars), multi-image, short video (<90s).
Hook formulas: F4 Time-Anchor Confession, F3 Year-over-Year Pivot, F9 Curiosity-Gap,
F11 Emotional Cold-Open.

### 3. Community / conversation (20-30%)

Reply-to-a-peer posts (answer a named person's post with your own finding — the format
that reaches the poster's network too), polls, spotlights, genuine questions.

Hook formulas: F6 Comment-Gate (sparingly, real deliverable only), F12 Permission
Slip, F14 Named Gratitude, poll posts.

### 4. Offer / product (max 1 post/week, 10-15%)

A soft offer tied to the week's topic, framed as something the reader can ignore.
Overuse kills trust.

Hook formulas: F2 R.I.P. Obituary (category pivots), F1 Anaphora (product as fix),
F13 Bait-and-Switch (upgrade announcements).

## Weekly guardrails

- 3-5 pillars in use max; no pillar above 60% of the week's posts
- Spread the goal across the week: at least one post each for saves, comments,
  reposts, likes — a week that is all comment-bait reads as engineered and flattens
- Content-to-engagement ratio: roughly 1 own post per 3-5 comments on others' posts;
  the comment lane is where inbound conversations start
- One post per slot, never 2+/day (cannibalization signal)

## Growth-stage playbook (by follower count)

- **<1k followers:** narrative-heavy (60%), comment lane does the reach lifting,
  3 posts/week max
- **1-10k:** default mix above; start recycling top posts after 10-12 weeks
- **10k+:** add carousels and native articles for the authority pillar; offer pillar
  still max 1/week
