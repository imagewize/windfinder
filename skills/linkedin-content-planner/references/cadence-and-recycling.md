# Cadence, timing, recycling and measurement — adapted from sergebulaev/linkedin-skills (MIT, pinned 8f8bc4b), condensed

## Timing

| Audience | Best window (audience local time) |
|---|---|
| US B2B / founders | Tue 8:00 AM ET, Wed 10:00 AM ET |
| EU decision-makers | Tue/Wed 7:00-8:30 AM CET |
| Global mixed | Tue/Wed/Thu 7:30-9:00 AM |

Avoid: Mon before 9 AM, Fri after 2 PM, Sat/Sun (B2B reach drops 30-50%).

## First 60-90 minutes

The "momentum window" determines most of a post's total reach:

- Reply to every comment within 90 minutes of posting
- If 3+ substantive comments land in the first 30 minutes, the post gets a second
  distribution boost
- Seed one substantive self-reply if the first hour is empty — never a bare link or
  "thanks!"
- Plan the day's comment lane for the same window

## Recycling protocol

- Re-run a top performer only after **10-12 weeks**, and only with a changed wrapper:
  new hook line, new dated fact or number, refreshed or reused visual
- Never re-run verbatim — recycled text is detectable and downranked
- Log every re-run as a new tracker row with "re-run of <date>" in the notes

## Measurement rules (load-bearing)

- **Record the age next to every number.** Reach keeps accruing for weeks: a 3-day
  figure understates a post by roughly 2x, a same-day figure by ~3x. Compare posts
  only at equal ages.
- Standard read points per post: **1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 30 days**
- Record: impressions, reactions, comments, reposts, profile views, followers gained,
  and the exact posting time
- Reach is diagnostic, not the goal. The goal column is pipeline: replies in the
  thread, DMs, profile visits from the target audience, offers accepted. A post with
  3x the impressions but zero target-audience engagement is the worse post.

## Comment-lane targeting

- Host posts under 24h old with roughly 20-100 comments: early, substantive comments
  here reach the most
- 100+ comment threads: only worth it in the first 1-2 hours while you can still be
  near the top; late comments there get almost nothing
- Prefer hosts in the target audience's lane (for a B2B offer: the buyers' feed, not
  the peers' feed) and slightly larger accounts whose readers are your audience
- Max 5 comments/day on own-account lanes, 15-20 connection requests/week; never
  automate comments (ToS enforcement + generic comments underperform anyway)
