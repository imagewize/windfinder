## Skeletons (F1-F10 condensed; F17-F20 in the upstream file)

- **F1:** "{Platform} can {throttle} you {timing}. [4-5 anaphoric lines, escalating
  specificity] You're renting {attention}. [Concrete anecdote with a real number in
  line 1-2] So I changed how I work: — [tactic] — [tactic]. [Plain-sentence reframe, no
  "Here's what most people miss"] [One-sentence product mention] [Question close]"
- **F2:** "R.I.P. {category}. Cause of death: {mechanism + numbers}. [Dated evidence]
  I defended {old thing} through {year}. It worked until {pivotal event + date}. Under
  the hood, {N} things changed: 1. … [One-line close]"
- **F3:** "{N} years ago I {X}. Today I {Y}. [What changed, with dates] [What it means
  for the reader] [Question]"
- **F4:** "{Date}: {specific uncomfortable fact, stated flat}. [What happened] [What I
  changed] [Question]" — never "Let me be honest" / "Confession:" as framing.
- **F5:** "I'm doing {commitment} in public for {period}. [Why] [The rule I set for
  myself] [First result or checkpoint with number] [Question]"
- **F6:** only with a real named deliverable; never "comment X to get Y".
- **F7:** "{Odd-precision number} is what {thing} cost me. [Breakdown: line items with
  numbers] [What I'd do differently] [Question]" — real numbers only.
- **F8:** "I almost paid {amount} for {tool}. Then I found {free thing}. [Comparison
  with numbers] [When the paid one is worth it] [Question]"
- **F9:** "{Provocative specific observation}. [The gap, paid off in ≤2 lines with a
  number] [What it means] [Question]" — no "what nobody tells you" teasers.
- **F10:** "{Widely believed claim}. {Historical receipt: date + what actually happened}.
  [What it means now] [Question]"
- **F17:** "I ran {A} vs {B} with one variable changed. [Setup] A: {result}. B:
  {result}. [Why, with a number] [Question]"
- **F18:** "{Option A} doesn't work because {dated fact}. {Option B} doesn't either.
  [The third option with a number] [Question]"
- **F19:** "I noticed {personal specific}. Turns out it was measured already: → {number}
  → {number} → {number}. [What to do with it] [Question]" — real numbers only.
- **F20:** "{A}: {trajectory over time}. {B}: {opposite trajectory}. Month one, X. Month
  six, Y. [One-line maxim]"

## Density rule

Top human creators still use these devices — the penalty is autopilot density plus
missing specifics, not the device:

- **One contrast per post.** A second "not X, it's Y" is the tell.
- **One triple per post**, and it must be three sourced numbers or three named acts.
- **Zero reveal bridges.** Write the reveal as a plain sentence with a number in it.
- **Zero questions before the close.**
- **Every abstract line pays for itself with a specific within two lines.**

## Hook micro-rules

- Number first, not question first; if the hook is a question, invert it into the
  number that answers it and move the question to the close
- "How I" beats "How to" (2-3x) — first-person experience over generic instruction
- A specific number in the first sentence raises expand-rate ~35% ($873.47 beats $900)
- A real failure in the first 3 lines, stated as a dated fact, outperforms polished
  framing ~8.5x

## Never do

- Blend two hooks in one post
- Open with a question, "Here's what/how", or "Stop X, start Y"
- Stack two contrasts or two triples
- Announce candor without a specific dated fact (false vulnerability)
- Use F6 more than once a month, or with "comment X to get Y"
- Pair F7 with made-up numbers
- Use F1 to frame LinkedIn as inferior
