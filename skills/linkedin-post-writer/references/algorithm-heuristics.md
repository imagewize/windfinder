# 2026 LinkedIn posting heuristics — adapted from sergebulaev/linkedin-skills references/algorithm-heuristics.md (MIT, pinned 8f8bc4b), condensed

Synthesis of the 360Brew paper (arXiv 2501.16450), AuthoredUp 2026 reach data, Trust
Insights Q1 2026, and reporting on LinkedIn's 2026 anti-pod/anti-slop measures.

## Timing

| Audience | Best window (local) |
|---|---|
| US B2B / founders | Tue 8:00 AM ET, Wed 10:00 AM ET |
| EU decision-makers | Tue/Wed 7:00-8:30 AM CET |
| Global mixed | Tue/Wed/Thu 7:30-9:00 AM, audience timezone |

Avoid Mon before 9 AM, Fri after 2 PM, Sat/Sun (B2B reach cut 30-50%).

## Format reach (relative to single image)

| Format | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Document carousel (PDF) | 1.7-2.3x |
| Native video (<90s, captioned, vertical) | 1.4-1.8x |
| Text-only | 1.0-1.3x |
| Poll | 1.1x |
| Single image | 1.0x baseline |
| External link in body | 0.4-0.6x |

Multi-image posts (3-4 images) show the highest absolute engagement rate (~6.6%) in
2026 benchmarks.

## Length and hook

- Sweet spot 900-1,300 chars; long-form 1,500-1,900 works with a line break every 1-2
  sentences and a narrative payoff; avoid <400 chars unless an established voice
- Hook cutoff: first **210 chars** (mobile "… see more")
- Hard limit: 3,000 chars

## Hashtags

- 0 hashtags performs equal to or better than 5+ (feed matching is semantic, not tag-based)
- 1-3 niche hashtags (<50k posts) give ~5% marginal lift
- 5+ hashtags correlate with spam patterns
- Placement: end of post, never mid-sentence

## Link placement

- In-body link: suppressed ~40-60%
- First comment: ~2.1x impressions vs in-body
- If the project bans links entirely, tell the story natively and let the profile carry
  the path

## First 60 minutes

- The first 60-90 min determine ~80% of total reach
- Author replies to every comment within 90 min
- 3+ substantive comments in the first 30 min → second distribution boost

## Penalties to avoid

- Comment pods / coordinated engagement: detection claimed at ~97%, penalty shadowban
  3-14 days, reach cut 60-90%
- Recycled reply templates on your own post: lexical-similarity downranking
- Over-posting: 2+ posts/day triggers cannibalization
- Engagement-bait CTAs and AI-slop patterns: named in LinkedIn's 2026 authenticity
  updates; reported posts lose ~40% of views

## Signal weights (reported, not officially confirmed)

- Save ≈ 5x a like, 2x a comment; in-depth comments beat one-word reactions ~4x
- Dwell time sweet spot 31-60s; "see more" expand + abandon <3s is a clickbait penalty

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Hook in first 210 chars, statement or number first, no question opener
- [ ] Length in the chosen band; 1-2 sentence paragraphs, blank lines between ideas
- [ ] One contrast, one sourced triple, zero reveal bridges
- [ ] 0-2 hashtags at the end (or project rule)
- [ ] No external link in the body (or project rule: link in first comment / no link)
- [ ] At least one specific number, one named entity, one first-person detail
- [ ] Closes with one specific question
- [ ] Visual attached if planned + alt text written
- [ ] Posting time in the audience's morning window; exact time recorded
- [ ] First 90 minutes free to reply to comments
