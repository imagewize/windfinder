# Repurposing: source → native LinkedIn post — adapted from sergebulaev/linkedin-skills linkedin-repurposer (MIT, pinned 8f8bc4b), condensed

Repurposing is not copy-paste. A post that flew on another platform flops pasted into
LinkedIn: wrong length, wrong rhythm, maybe a link in the body that tanks reach. Keep
the **claim and the facts**, rebuild the **delivery**.

## Steps

1. **Take the source** (blog post, case study, thread, video script, caption, tweet) and
   the goal (comments / reposts / likes / saves).
2. **Extract the spine**: the single claim, story, or number worth keeping. Repurposing
   fails when it keeps the words instead of the point.
3. **Re-hook for LinkedIn**: new first line from `hook-formulas.md`, picked by goal, in
   the first 210 chars. The source's hook rarely survives.
4. **Expand to LinkedIn length**: medium 900-1,300 chars (default). A dense tweet
   becomes 4-6 short paragraphs with blank lines, one concrete detail per beat — not a
   wall, not a list.
5. **Add the LinkedIn shape**: whitespace between ideas, one moment of real stakes or
   vulnerability, one specific closing question.
6. **Fix links and artifacts**: no external link in the body (first comment if the
   project allows); strip hashtag walls, "link in bio", "smash subscribe", platform
   handles, "as I tweeted" throat-clearing. 0-2 hashtags at the end.
7. **Scrub** with `ai-tell-scrub.md`.
8. **Approval card** per SKILL.md.

## Source → LinkedIn rules

| Source | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tweet | One tweet is a hook; grow the argument underneath with whitespace. Expand, never paste. |
| X/thread | Unroll into one flowing post, not a numbered list; keep the best line as the hook. |
| Blog / newsletter / case study | Pick the single most quotable claim as the hook, then the one story or test that proves it. Do not summarize the whole piece. |
| Video / script | Lead with the payoff, then the story of how you got there. Video link in first comment if allowed. |
| Instagram/TikTok caption | Strip emoji density and hashtag blocks; add the professional stakes. |
| Performance data / tracker row | Re-run after 10-12 weeks with a new hook and a new dated fact; log as a new row. |

## Hard rules

- Keep the source's claim and facts intact — repurposing changes delivery, never meaning
  or numbers
- Never paste and trim; rebuild hook, length and rhythm from the spine
- No external link in the body
- At least one moment of real stakes; keep real numbers and named entities
- One product/self mention max, only as the natural conclusion

## Anti-patterns (refuse)

- Light-edit copy-paste of the source
- Source-platform artifacts left in
- Tweet-length post with no whitespace or expansion
- All-caps first line
- Meta throat-clearing ("I originally posted this on...")
